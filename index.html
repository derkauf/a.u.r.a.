A.U.R.A. · Spatial home control

Before you start

A.U.R.A. puts the controls of your home devices in the room with you, in mixed reality on Meta Quest. Each device has a printed tag; look at it and its controls appear right there.

1. Allow the headset camera

To read the printed tags, A.U.R.A. needs the headset camera. Allow it now, before entering AR. In the prompt, make sure Headset cameras is on (Virtual cameras alone only shows your avatar, and the image stays black).

Missed it? Settings › Privacy & safety › Browser › Headset cameras, or the lock icon next to the address.

2. Tags (optional)

Print the tags and stick them next to your devices: 1 lamp, 2 air conditioner, 3 door lock, 10 TV. No tags? You can still try everything with the tag icons in the app.

Printable tags (PDF) User guide (PDF)

3. In the app

  1. Enter AR when the browser offers it.
  2. Pinch (or pull the trigger) on the scan button next to the gear, and look at a tag from about half a meter.
  3. The device's controls appear above its tag. Pinch the tag to open or close them.
